name: Accounting and insight boundaries
description: Product decisions governing material outcomes, comparisons, and constrained AI.
---
Rework means physical material set aside at closure, not cumulative processing throughput. Reusable offcuts are accepted for future use, not confirmed consumed in a subsequent job.

**Why:** Counting throughput or assuming actual reuse would double-count material and misrepresent scrap.

**How to apply:** Keep each kilogram in one closure outcome. Do not present a baseline gap as proven avoidable waste or an established cause.

AI summaries use evidence-locked numeric sentences and model-selected, allowlisted investigation prompts.

**Why:** The user explicitly prohibits invented causes and model-controlled calculations. Restricted generation is a deliberate reliability tradeoff, not a missing general-purpose chatbot.

**How to apply:** Preserve these constraints when expanding insight generation; label model-selected guidance accurately as AI-assisted.
