## AI

Do NOT let AI control calculations.

Calculations, balance, percentages, comparisons, and attention flags must come from deterministic application code.

Add a simple "Generate Insight" action only after the core workflow works.

The AI should receive validated metrics, scrap breakdown, comparison data, and explicitly identified user-entered notes.

The AI output should:

* summarize the actual data
* identify the largest recorded loss category
* explain the comparison when available
* suggest one or two investigation prompts
* state limitations when evidence is incomplete

The AI must NOT:

* invent root causes
* blame an operator
* claim a machine is defective
* create unsupported financial claims
* recalculate the business metrics independently
* turn a correlation into a proven cause

If AI generation fails, show a deterministic fallback summary.

The summary must follow the selected language.

Do not spend excessive development effort on AI before the core application is complete.
